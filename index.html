✦ About Us What Sankalp is and why it exists +

Sankalp is a student-focused study and productivity platform designed to make everyday academic planning simpler. It brings goals, milestones, schedules, focused study sessions, progress tracking, and study documents into one organized space.

The idea behind Sankalp is simple: students should be able to concentrate on their work instead of spending unnecessary time managing different tools. Sankalp helps turn a larger academic goal into smaller steps that can be planned, worked on, and reviewed over time.

Sankalp is designed with a local-first approach. Core information such as goals, sessions, settings, check-ins, and supported documents can be kept in the browser on the device where you use Sankalp. This approach is intended to give students more control over their study information and reduce the need to send personal study data to a remote service.

Sankalp is an independent student project. Features may continue to change as the project grows, and feedback from students can help shape future improvements.

⌾ Privacy Policy How Sankalp handles your information +

Your privacy matters to Sankalp. This Privacy Policy explains the general way information is handled when you use the Sankalp website and its study-management features.

Information you create

Sankalp may store information that you intentionally enter or create while using the application. This can include goals, milestones, timetable entries, study-session records, check-ins, progress information, application preferences, and documents that you choose to add.

Local storage

Sankalp's core local features are designed to store supported information in browser storage on your device. This means that the information can remain available when you return to the same website using the same browser and device.

Local storage also has an important limitation: it is controlled by your browser and device. Clearing website data, clearing browser storage, using private browsing, changing browsers, resetting a device, browser storage limits, or other technical changes can make locally stored information unavailable.

Documents and study files

Documents added through Sankalp's document feature are intended to remain in browser storage on your device rather than being uploaded to a Sankalp server as part of normal local document storage. Because browser storage is not a permanent backup service, important files should also be kept in a separate backup location.

Accounts and passwords

Sankalp's core features do not require a traditional Sankalp account or Sankalp password. You should never enter passwords, banking information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information into fields that do not require it.

Analytics and third-party services

Sankalp's local productivity features are designed so that study information does not need to be sent to a Sankalp server simply to calculate progress, manage goals, or display locally stored information. The website may nevertheless use hosting, browser, analytics, or other third-party technologies depending on the version of the site. Those services can have their own privacy policies and data practices.

Email communication

If you use Contact Us, Sankalp opens your email application using a mail link. The message is then handled by your chosen email provider. Any information you decide to include in the email is information you voluntarily send.

Changes to this policy

Sankalp may update this policy when features, technology, or data practices change. The version displayed on the current Sankalp website should be treated as the latest version.

? FAQs Answers to common questions +
Is Sankalp free?

Sankalp is designed as a free student productivity project. Features and availability may change as the project develops.

Do I need a Sankalp account?

The core local productivity features do not require a traditional Sankalp account. Supported information can be stored locally by your browser.

Where are my documents stored?

Documents added through the document feature are intended to be stored in browser storage on your device. Browser storage limits vary by device and browser.

Sankalp is designed to work with supported modern browsers, including mobile browsers. The exact experience can vary depending on your device and browser.

▣ Data & Storage Understand your local data +

Sankalp uses a local-first approach for its core study features. Goals, study sessions, settings, check-ins, and supported documents can be stored by the browser on your device.

You can use the Export JSON option in Settings to create a backup of supported application data. Keep important backups somewhere safe, especially before clearing browser data, changing browsers, or moving to another device.

Local storage is normally specific to the browser and device where it was created. Your Sankalp information should therefore not be assumed to automatically synchronize between your phone, laptop, and other devices.
