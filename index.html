ABOUT SANKALP

About Us

Learn about Sankalp, your privacy, local data storage, and how to reach us.

✦ About Us What Sankalp is and why it exists +

Sankalp is an independent, student-focused productivity web application designed to make everyday academic planning simpler. It integrates daily study targets, timetable management, focused stopwatch sessions, multi-track syllabus tracking, and study document organization into a single cohesive interface.

Built with a privacy-first, local-first philosophy, your study metrics, timetable entries, and files are stored directly in your browser on your device using Web Storage and IndexedDB.

⌾ Privacy Policy How Sankalp handles your information +

Sankalp does not collect, sell, or transmit your study logs, goals, or documents to external databases. Information created remains in your browser's local client storage.

Local Storage Disclaimer

Because data resides on your current browser and device, clearing browser cache or website data will remove your local records. Use the Export JSON tool in Settings to keep regular backup files.

✓ Terms & Conditions Rules for using Sankalp +

Sankalp is provided as a free student organizational utility. It does not replace official school syllabi, instructions, or examination authorities. Users remain responsible for validating examination dates and backing up critical study files.

? FAQs Answers to common questions +
Is Sankalp free to use?

Yes. Sankalp is built as an accessible, free productivity platform for students.

Do I need to sign up for an account?

No account or password is required. The platform operates immediately using local browser storage.

Can I use Sankalp on mobile?

Yes. Sankalp is fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers, and can be installed via your browser menu as a standalone web app.

▣ Data & Storage Understand your local data +

Sankalp utilizes localStorage for configuration and goals, alongside IndexedDB for local file storage up to 100 MB per document. Export your JSON archive anytime in Settings to transfer data between devices.
