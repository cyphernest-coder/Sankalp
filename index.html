✦ About Us What Sankalp is and why it exists +

Sankalp is an independent, student-focused productivity web application designed to make everyday academic planning simpler.

It combines daily study targets, timetable management, focused stopwatch sessions, subject and chapter tracking, exam preparation, and study document organization into one workspace.

⌾ Privacy Policy How Sankalp handles your information +

Sankalp does not require an account for its core functionality. Study information is designed to remain in browser storage on the current device.

Local Storage Disclaimer

Clearing browser data may remove local records. Use the Export JSON feature in Settings to maintain backups.

✓ Terms & Conditions Rules for using Sankalp +

Sankalp is provided as a free student organizational utility. It does not replace official school syllabi, examination authorities, or institutional instructions.

? FAQs Answers to common questions +
Is Sankalp free to use?

Yes. Sankalp is designed as a free student productivity platform.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the core local-first experience.

Can I use Sankalp on mobile?

Yes. The interface is designed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile browsers.

▣ Data & Storage Understand your local data +

Sankalp uses browser storage for application data and IndexedDB for local study files. Export your JSON archive from Settings to maintain a backup.
